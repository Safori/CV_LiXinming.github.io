2026IEDM

First Demonstration of Oxide-Semiconductor Dual-Gate Polarization-Boosted Charge-Trapping Flash: Comprehensive Modeling and Experimental Validation

Xiaoqing Sun, Xinming Li, Dong Zhang, et al.

IEEE International Electron Devices Meeting (IEDM 2026)Co-first author

We propose the first oxide-semiconductor dual-gate polarization-boosted charge-trapping flash, with a comprehensive model coupling antiferroelectric polarization switching, tunneling, and charge trapping. The device achieves a 10.3 V memory window under ±13 V operation, keeps 6.9 V after 10^5 program/erase cycles, and shows projected 10-year retention.

Accepted Sep 2026 · to be presented Dec 12–16, 2026, San Francisco

2026VLSI

BEOL-Compatible Oxide Semiconductor Twin-Gate FeFET Achieving a Record-High Memory Window of 25.4 V and a Near-Ideal Memory Window-to-Operation Voltage Ratio of 1.88

Xinming Li, Ning Ji, Leming Jiao, et al.

IEEE Symposium on VLSI Technology and Circuits

We demonstrate a twin-gate architecture for BEOL-compatible oxide-semiconductor FeFETs with channel lengths down to 30 nm, achieving a record-high memory window of 25.4 V at 13.5 V operation (memory-window-to-operation-voltage ratio 1.88, near the theoretical limit) while retaining over 15 V after 10⁷ cycles.

All authors

Xinming Li, Ning Ji, Leming Jiao, Yi Tong, Gan Liu, Chaoming Wu, Trinh Duy Hieu, Zhilun Zhang, Jiawei Sun, Kai Ni, Gengchiau Liang, Chen Sun, Dong Zhang, Xiao Gong

2026IEEE EDL

Multi-Grain Ferroelectric Induced Drain Current Variability in BEOL-Compatible OS FeFETs

Wei Zhang, Chen Sun, Xinming Li, et al.

IEEE Electron Device Letters

We study intrinsic variability in BEOL-compatible ZnO-channel OS FeFETs scaled to 40 nm, finding drain-current variation rises monotonically under full switching but non-monotonically under partial switching, driven by stochastic percolation and ferroelectric grain inhomogeneity.

All authors

Wei Zhang, Chen Sun, Xinming Li, Zixuan Wang, Jianze Wang, Yunuo Cen, Dong Zhang, Tao Du, Ye Lu, Xiao Gong, Xuanyao Fong

2025IEDM

First Demonstration of Oxide Semiconductor BEOL-Compatible Dual-Gate FeFET Achieving a Record-Low Operation Voltage of 0.5 V and a Record-High Memory Window-to-Operation Voltage Ratio of 1.62

Dong Zhang, Xinming Li, Xiaopeng Li, et al.

IEEE International Electron Devices MeetingCo-first author

Using gate-area-ratio engineering, we demonstrate a dual-gate OS FeFET operating at a record-low 0.5 V with endurance over 10⁹ cycles, and a record-high memory-window-to-operation-voltage ratio of 1.62 at 1.25 V.

2025IEDM

First Demonstration of 3D Fe-VNAND with Function-Separated Dual-Layer Oxide Semiconductor Channel and Laminated Ferroelectric Stack

Chen Sun, Xinming Li, Dong Zhang, et al.

IEEE International Electron Devices Meeting

We demonstrate the first 3D ferroelectric vertical NAND with a dual-layer oxide-semiconductor channel and a laminated ferroelectric stack, reaching a 9.6 V memory window in the 3D string with endurance and retention beyond 10⁹ cycles.

A Generalizable Tri-Layer Design Framework for Enhancing OSFET Reliability

Gan Liu, Qiwen Kong, Jingguang Lu, Xinming Li, et al.

IEEE Symposium on VLSI Technology and Circuits

We propose a tri-layer design that tackles positive and negative bias temperature instability together, using a hydrogen-reservoir top layer and a hydrogen-barrier passivation layer to reach a record-low threshold-voltage shift in IGZTO FETs.

2024ACS ANM

Toward Nanoscale Organic Tunnel Field-Effect Transistors with Small Subthreshold Swing and High On-State Current: A Computational Design Based on Two-Dimensional Covalent-Organic Frameworks

Xinming Li, Yuan Li

ACS Applied Nano Materials

Using first-principles and quantum-transport calculations, we design nanoscale organic tunnel FETs based on two-dimensional covalent-organic frameworks with subthreshold swing down to 21 mV/dec and on-state current up to 887.5 μA/μm, meeting IRDS targets for high-performance and low-power devices.
