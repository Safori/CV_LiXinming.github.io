Selected publications

2026VLSI

BEOL-Compatible Oxide Semiconductor Twin-Gate FeFET Achieving a Record-High Memory Window of 25.4 V and a Near-Ideal Memory Window-to-Operation Voltage Ratio of 1.88

Xinming Li, Ning Ji, Leming Jiao, et al.

IEEE Symposium on VLSI Technology and Circuits

We demonstrate a twin-gate architecture for BEOL-compatible oxide-semiconductor FeFETs with channel lengths down to 30 nm, achieving a record-high memory window of 25.4 V at 13.5 V operation (memory-window-to-operation-voltage ratio 1.88, near the theoretical limit) while retaining over 15 V after 10⁷ cycles.

Publisher
All authors

Xinming Li, Ning Ji, Leming Jiao, Yi Tong, Gan Liu, Chaoming Wu, Trinh Duy Hieu, Zhilun Zhang, Jiawei Sun, Kai Ni, Gengchiau Liang, Chen Sun, Dong Zhang, Xiao Gong

2026IEEE EDL

Multi-Grain Ferroelectric Induced Drain Current Variability in BEOL-Compatible OS FeFETs

Wei Zhang, Chen Sun, Xinming Li, et al.

IEEE Electron Device Letters

We study intrinsic variability in BEOL-compatible ZnO-channel OS FeFETs scaled to 40 nm, finding drain-current variation rises monotonically under full switching but non-monotonically under partial switching, driven by stochastic percolation and ferroelectric grain inhomogeneity.

Publisher
All authors

Wei Zhang, Chen Sun, Xinming Li, Zixuan Wang, Jianze Wang, Yunuo Cen, Dong Zhang, Tao Du, Ye Lu, Xiao Gong, Xuanyao Fong

2025IEDM

First Demonstration of Oxide Semiconductor BEOL-Compatible Dual-Gate FeFET Achieving a Record-Low Operation Voltage of 0.5 V and a Record-High Memory Window-to-Operation Voltage Ratio of 1.62

Dong Zhang, Xinming Li, Xiaopeng Li, et al.

IEEE International Electron Devices MeetingCo-first author

Using gate-area-ratio engineering, we demonstrate a dual-gate OS FeFET operating at a record-low 0.5 V with endurance over 10⁹ cycles, and a record-high memory-window-to-operation-voltage ratio of 1.62 at 1.25 V.

All authors
